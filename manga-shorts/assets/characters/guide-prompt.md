# 案内役キャラ「バタパパ（仮）」— 忙しそうなパパ

## 設定
- 30代前半の新米パパ。育休中で、家事と育児に毎日バタバタしている。
- 根はまじめで段取り好き。手が足りない場面を、道具と仕組みで乗り切っていく。
- 見た目の固定ポイント（毎回同じにする）: 短い黒髪で寝ぐせが1房はねている／少し疲れた目元（薄いクマ）／紺のパーカー／グレーのエプロン／肩にガーゼのタオル。

## Gemini に貼る3面図プロンプト

```
Character reference sheet for a Japanese manga, clean black and white line art with soft screentone, plain white background.
Show the SAME character three times side by side: front view, side view (facing left), back view. Full body, standing, neutral pose. Add one extra close-up of the face (front) with a tired but kind smile.
Character: a busy new dad in his early 30s, Japanese, average build, 175 cm.
Fixed features: short black hair with one cowlick sticking up at the top, slightly tired eyes with faint dark circles, navy hoodie, plain gray apron, a white gauze towel over his left shoulder, dark pants, house slippers.
Simple, friendly manga style, easy to redraw consistently. Absolutely NO text, letters or labels anywhere.
```
